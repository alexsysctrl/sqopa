Statecraft

A world that keeps running on its own — nations with economies, borders that move for reasons, wars that leave something behind, leaders who change because of who came before them. It runs on a schedule, with a map you can look at.

The next thing on it is a game you play: a country, other people playing theirs, and the engine keeping the rest of the world honest while you decide.

Alt-rock

An engine that writes and records songs in that style — guitars, bass, drums and a room, all of it built from scratch every time. No samples, no loops, nothing loaded from a library. It reads a recording first: the tempo, the key, which strings are ringing, then it plays a band around it.

Used for Sucker Robot's records. Three songs done, more to go.

Smallrock

A model that makes music the way a spectrogram is shaped rather than the way a sentence is typed — an array over time and frequency and two channels, with a learned compressor in front of it. Early. It works on two seconds at a time and nothing about it is borrowed from anyone else's model.

Cerebrum

A small language model, studied honestly: what changes an architecture and what only looks like it does. Positions, attention span, mixture-of-experts, memory that retrieves — the findings are written up with the numbers and the seeds, including the parts that turned out to be nothing.
